About

A new way to make a sneaker yours.

KickCanvas is building a new way to create sneakers — the freedom to choose the colours, materials and details that make a pair yours. It started with a simple frustration: customisation tools online mostly show you a flat drawing and ask you to imagine the rest. So we built the opposite — a configurator around a real 3D model, where the colour you pick is the colour the material actually takes.

The configurator is live and orders are open. Every order is paid by Cash on Delivery and confirmed by our team before your pair is made.

What KickCanvas is

Who it’s for

Students and anyone who’d rather not wear the same pair as everyone in the room.

How it’ll be made

Nothing is manufactured in advance. Each pair is produced to order once our team confirms it.

Where we’ll ship

Across India, from Bengaluru (Bangalore), with tracking from the moment your design is confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I design my own custom sneakers on KickCanvas?

Open the 3D configurator, pick one of three silhouettes, then tap any part of the shoe — upper, panels, laces, tongue, heel, midsole or outsole — and give it a colour. Pick one finish, such as matte, gloss or suede, and it applies to the whole sneaker. You can add your name or upload artwork and drag it into place on the actual 3D surface. Nothing is charged and no account is needed.

Can I order a pair now?

Yes. Add your design to the cart and place a Cash on Delivery order. Every order is reviewed by the KickCanvas team and is only confirmed once we approve it; you pay in cash when your pair arrives.

How much will a pair of custom sneakers cost?

The three silhouettes are priced from ₹4,499 to ₹5,299. Each recoloured part, a premium finish and added artwork or text carry a small charge, shown live in the configurator as you build.

Can I put my own logo or artwork on the shoe?

Yes. The configurator takes a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG upload and projects it onto the real 3D surface, so it follows the curve of the material and is lit with the rest of the shoe. You can move, rotate and resize it, and place up to four prints per shoe.

Does the 3D sneaker customizer work on a phone?

Yes. The 3D model responds to drag to rotate and pinch to zoom, and tapping a part of the shoe selects it. It has been tested down to a 360 px screen.

Where does KickCanvas ship?

Across India, from Bengaluru (Bangalore), with tracking from the moment a design is confirmed. Each pair is made to order — nothing is manufactured in advance.
